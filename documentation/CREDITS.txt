HISTORICAL DATA

Railway placement and construction-date references are based in part on the Historical Canadian Railroads / Historical_Railways_1836_1922_05Dec09.shp dataset, created by the Cartography office, Geography Department, and made available via MDL Data / GEORIA / Borealis.

License: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/

Dataset identifier: doi:10.5683/SP2/UCCFVQ

The dataset documentation cites, among other sources:
- Christopher Andreae, Lines of Country: An Atlas of Railway and Waterway History in Canada. Erin, Ontario: Boston Mills Press, 1997.
- National Atlas of Canada / GeoGratis base map railway data.
- ESRI Canada ArcCANADA data.

Modified and adapted for use in this mod.
