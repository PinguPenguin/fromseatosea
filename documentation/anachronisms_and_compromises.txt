1. Most Canadian colonies in 1836 would have used the Union Jack, not their in-game flags.
    This comes from vanilla and is kept for gameplay. The existing colonial flags are not historical 1836 flags; most are PDX's placeholders, and PEI's is from c2c.

2. Several state and country names are anachronistically modern (i.e. Quebec, Ontario, etc.) - This is so you can actually read the map, lol

3. Some hubs are past the timeframe of Vic3 (Happy Valley, Matagami, Thompson, etc.).
    These are an abstraction, since we need a single "point" to model resource extraction (like mining in N Manitoba). None of these are too egregious; if you insist, you can headcanon it as your excellent stewardship of Canada making it surpass real life timelines. :)
    Other hubs that were founded later can also be found (i.e. Calgary), but aren't as bad.

4. Many port hubs aren't where the actual largest port is (Halifax/Sydney, Vancouver/New Westminster, etc.)
    I would love to make St. John's a thriving city-port, but unfortunately, the engine only allows me to set one hub per type.
    This current situation is me compromising, since Vancouver being a major city is more important than it being a port, I would say.
